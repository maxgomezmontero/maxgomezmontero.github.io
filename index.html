ProjectTracker — the visual command center I run my projects from

My role
Sole designer and developer. I built it on my own, for my own daily work as a project engineer, with AI coding agents (Claude Code).
What I did
A desktop app I work in all day: a board of every project, a Today digest of what changed, a Story timeline of each problem, a 180-day schedule with install clash alerts and a flow map of what is holding each job. Built in: rules that file ~145 emails a week to the right project, detect milestones, check layout drawings against 44 criteria and queue ERP updates for my approval. Rules decide; an LLM only writes one-line summaries.
Result
94% agreement with past manual decisions, ~7.3 h a week saved (~340 h/year, estimated), and drawing checks that caught real errors before release, such as a 350 vs 500 lb hook load.

Lean redesign proposal for a paint-mixing system assembly

My role
One of a team of 5 (M.S. coursework). My part: mapping the assembly workflow and its waste, redesigning the layout and designing the dedicated work cell.
What we did
Time study, value stream map and 5 Whys on the assembly of a two-component paint mixing system. From that we proposed an assembly cell with 5S, kanban + poka-yoke and an SOP.
Result
Only 15.5 of 666 lead-time hours added value. The redesign was proposed, not implemented: the team estimated ~$16.5K/year in savings for a $12.4K investment, a 9-month payback.

04 / Research

Machines that
predict their own quality.

Machine-learning research on checking part quality while the part is being made, instead of after.

Published · Springer · co-author

ANN-based online surface roughness prediction (ANN-OSRP) in wire EDM

Chen, J., Gomez Montero, M. A., & Yu, S. (2026). Neural Computing and Applications, 38(4), 87. doi.org/10.1007/s00521-025-11745-x ↗

A neural network that predicts wire-EDM surface roughness while the part is being cut, from 12 online features built from the controller's voltage, current and feed rate. Trained and tested on 81 cuts of 2507 duplex steel on a Sodick VZ-300L.

~95.8%
average prediction accuracy
