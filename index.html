ProjectTracker — engineering workflow automation

Jun 2026 – present · Dove Equipment / ICAFe

A desktop app that runs a project engineer's day: email, ERP, drawings and deadlines in one place.

Problem

Managing ~20–25 live projects meant hours each week matching emails to jobs, updating the ERP by hand and catching drawing errors late.

What I built

  • Integration with Outlook, Acumatica ERP and SharePoint
  • Email-to-project matching and milestone detection (rules + LLM reading of threads) across ~145 emails/week
  • Automated drawing QA (44-criteria check) and drawing-vs-quote cross-checks
  • Index of 1,697 equipment items from 620 archived projects

Results

  • 94% agreement with past manual decisions
  • Caught real errors before release (e.g. 350 vs 500 lb hook load)
  • ~7.3 h/week saved (~340 h/year)
  • Every ERP write is human-approved
