Operations & logistics: time studies to digital workflows

Before plants and software: timing every warehouse process to size the crew, and leading a digital transformation of order-to-delivery workflows.

Role
Logistics Analyst · IE final project lead
Where
Tucumán, Argentina
When
2023
Methods
Time studies · staffing models · KPIs · WMS/TMS
Status
Completed

Problem

A distribution warehouse sized its crew by feel, and order-to-delivery workflows ran on paper and phone calls.

What I did

Timed each warehouse process to build staffing models by weekday plus a KPI dashboard; separately, led a WMS/TMS digital transformation as my Industrial Engineering final project.

Result

+15% operational efficiency and +10% sales productivity per worker at the distributor; +15% perfect deliveries in the final project.

Warehouse time studiesIndustry 4.0 final projectResults

01Warehouse time studies

Logistics Analyst, ABC Distributions (Apr – Dec 2023). Raised operational efficiency 15% through time studies and workforce optimization; digital tools lifted sales productivity 10% per worker.

How: each process — receiving, putaway, replenishment, picking, order breakdown, returns — was broken into timed steps, turned into standard times, and multiplied by expected volumes to get the hours needed per weekday. A warehouse KPI dashboard tracked inventory accuracy, turnover and space use.
