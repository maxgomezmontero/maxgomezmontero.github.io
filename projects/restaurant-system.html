Restaurant accounting & analytics app

A Windows desktop app that turns a restaurant’s scattered sales data — POS ticket PDFs, three delivery portals and paper receipts — into payroll, cash reconciliation, monthly close and menu margins.

Role
Designer & developer
Team

Stack

Status
In daily use since Oct 2026

Problem

Sales lived in POS ticket PDFs, DoorDash, UberEats and GrubHub exports and paper receipts, so monthly close and menu costing were slow and manual.

What I did

Built a desktop app that imports every source into one ledger, OCRs receipts, and runs payroll, cash reconciliation, monthly close and menu analytics, with Excel/PDF reports.

Result

In daily use by the restaurant since October 2026, with auto-updating releases and cloud sync.

How it worksMenu marginsKey decisionsConfidentiality

01How it works

  1. Import. POS ticket PDFs and DoorDash / UberEats / GrubHub CSVs are parsed; paper receipts are read with OCR.
  2. Normalise. Every source becomes the same transaction format in a local SQLite database with cloud sync.
  3. Operate. Payroll, cash reconciliation and monthly close run on that single ledger.
  4. Analyse. Cost per dish, margin by item and trends; Excel and PDF reports for the owner and the accountant.

03Key decisions

Decision

One format for every source

Five very different inputs are converted once, so every report reads the same ledger.

Decision

Local-first, synced

SQLite on the restaurant’s PC keeps it fast and offline-safe; cloud sync keeps a copy.

Decision

Ship updates automatically

Auto-updating releases mean fixes reach the restaurant without a visit.

04Confidentiality

The restaurant’s data is private and the repository stays private. The demos use an invented menu and invented orders.
